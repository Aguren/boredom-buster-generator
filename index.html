Cipher Protocol Guide:

• A=1 Number: Beginner level. A=1, B=2, etc.

• Symbols/Pigpen: Visual puzzle matching symbols or grid lines to letters.

• Reverse: Words are spelled backward.

• Caesar Shift: Alphabet offset cipher for older kids.

What is Shift Amount?

A Caesar Cipher shifts every letter down the alphabet by a set number of steps.

• +1 Shift: A becomes B, B becomes C, Z becomes A.

• +3 Shift: A becomes D, B becomes E, etc.

Higher shifts make the puzzle slightly harder for older kids!
